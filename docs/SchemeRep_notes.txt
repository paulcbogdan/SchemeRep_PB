Here is a picture of the IRAF across all 246 regions for each stimulus. You can see how there is a "global" effect, wherein every region's IRAF is correlated with every other region's IRAF.

Why is this the case? For a given trial, the IRAF for region a for a given trial i is the correlation between vector A (fMRI) and vector Xi (stimulus). Calculating the correlation is mathematically equivalent to (1) standardizing vector A, such that the values are centered at zero and have a unit standard deviation, and (2) calculating the weighted average of A, with each scalar weighed by its corresponding value in Xi.
